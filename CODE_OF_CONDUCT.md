# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional and harassment-free community
for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent while evaluating technical claims with evidence.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar concepts without condescension.
- Respect privacy, licenses, security boundaries and confidential information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize and repair harm when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private data are
not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when needed
to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical and without retaliation.

This policy applies to project repositories, discussions, issue trackers,
review channels and public representation of the project.
